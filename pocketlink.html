Meet online.

Pocket Links connects accepted friends online. Both players need PocketLink Plus or existing tester access.

A note on linking.

Compatibility depends on the game, revision, and link protocol. Linked play uses a separate save session: ending the link restores your pre-link progress, including undoing trades and other changes made during the session.

PocketLink does not include games or a game download catalog.
