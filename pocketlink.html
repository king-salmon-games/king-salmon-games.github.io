Linked play

Play offline

Import game files you are authorized to use. Keep saves on your device and play offline without an account.

Link nearby

Each player brings their own compatible game file. Connect through Game Links with the host’s approval.

Meet online

Pocket Links connects accepted friends online. Both players need PocketLink Plus or existing tester access.

Link compatibility

Compatibility depends on the game, revision, and link protocol. Linked play uses a separate save session. Ending a link restores your pre-link progress, including undoing trades and other changes made during that session.

PocketLink does not include games or a game download catalog.
