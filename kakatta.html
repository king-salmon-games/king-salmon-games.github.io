Kakatta

One more cast.

A first-person fishing game for iPhone. Flick to cast, time your hook, and work the rod and reel to bring your catch out of the current.

  • In development
  • For iPhone
  • First-person fishing

A little patience. A quick reaction.

Follow the drift, find your moment, and stay with the fish. Every cast is another chance to land the catch you’ll remember.

01

Cast and connect.

Flick out your line, guide it as it sinks, then time the hook. The right moment makes all the difference.

02

Work the water.

Small phone tilts steer the rod while the camera stays steady. Hold to reel; release to give the fish line.

03

Chase your best.

Land your catch and keep separate records for weight and length. Then cast again.
