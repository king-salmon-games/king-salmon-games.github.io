WUMBOS Support

Stuck in the workday? Send a short description and we’ll help.

Contact support

Email bigkingsalmongames@gmail.com with:

  • WUMBOS app version
  • iOS version and device model
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead

Do not send passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal information.

TestFlight feedback

TestFlight testers can also use “Send Beta Feedback” inside TestFlight. Apple may attach screenshots, comments, and crash details to feedback you submit.

Resetting a save

Choose “Start New Wumbo” from the title screen to replace the current character and career. Delete the app to clear remaining local settings on the device, subject to Apple backup settings.
