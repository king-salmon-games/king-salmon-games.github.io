Game support

Need a hand with Cream Cheese × Phone, PocketLink, Wumbos, Kakatta, or Nemus Obscurum? Tell us what happened.

Contact support

Email bigkingsalmongames@gmail.com with:

  • Project name and app version
  • Operating-system version and device model
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead

Do not send passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal information.

TestFlight feedback

TestFlight testers can also use “Send Beta Feedback” inside TestFlight. Apple may attach screenshots, comments, and crash details to feedback you submit.

Resetting a Wumbos save

Choose “Start New Wumbo” from the title screen to replace the current character and career. Delete the app to clear remaining local settings on the device, subject to Apple backup settings.

River scene credits

Part of the homepage pool motion comes from Black Sea waves captured by stereo cameras. King Salmon Games cropped the measurements, trained a surface model, and scaled its waves to the river. Wave data by Guimarães and colleagues (Ifremer, 2019) · CC BY 4.0

The waterfall ribbons draw on high-speed footage of water flowing over a laboratory weir. King Salmon Games cropped and sampled the footage, fitted a model to the moving bright ribbons, and adapted their motion to the rock ledges. Falling-sheet footage by Lodomez and colleagues (2020) · CC BY 4.0
